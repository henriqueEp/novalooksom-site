About us

About Nova Look Som

We are a loudspeaker components factory located in Taboão da Serra, in the São Paulo metropolitan area, Brazil. For 28 years we have supplied voice coils and diaphragm assemblies to speaker manufacturers and audio stores in Brazil, and to customers in some Latin American countries.

We work to order: the customer sends a design or a sample and we produce it to their specification. We sell only to companies.

Products

What we manufacture

Loudspeaker voice coils

Single and dual voice coils for woofers, subwoofers and midrange speakers. Kapton (polyimide), aluminum or fiber formers, with copper, aluminum or CCAW wire, made to the customer's design.

Diaphragm assemblies for drivers and tweeters

Replacement assemblies for compression drivers, horns and super tweeters: diaphragm, voice coil and terminals mounted on the ring. Phenolic, polyimide or titanium diaphragms.

Drivers

We also supply complete drivers to our customers in Brazil.
